# Dossier Deltas — red-team adjudication of prior-art imports vs architecture v1.0

**Date:** 12 Jul 2026
**Inputs:** all 15 dossiers under `docs/prior_art/` (index: `docs/prior_art/README.md`), `docs/organizer_playbook.md`, `docs/architecture.md`, `docs/question_analysis.md`, `src/core/geometry/{toolbox,primitives}.py`, `src/core/calibration.py`, `reports/gt_battery_full_2026-07-11/gt_battery_report.md`, `docs/cvsweep_rerun_brief.md`.
**Provenance rule applied:** per `docs/prior_art/README.md` header, primary-sourced dossier facts (code read directly, generation code verified) outrank summaries; team docs stay authoritative for build/process questions.

## Verdict

Architecture v1.0 survives the dossier confrontation structurally intact — the winner's shape (NROS: scene graph + multi-modal frontier scoring, `docs/prior_art/2025_1st_nros.md`) and the organizer reference design (SORT3D toolbox, `docs/prior_art/sort3d.md`) both corroborate our deterministic-backbone/checkpointed-LLM bet, and every 2025 pipeline that leaned on GT `/object_markers` (ReasonX, CopyPasta ×2/3 of its stack, Jana, Anand Singh) is structurally inapplicable in 2026, which strengthens rather than threatens our own-perception decision. The real damage is in §A: our geometry toolbox's `on`, `near`, `between`, `next_to`, `with`, and `under` predicates use **different functional forms** from the VLA-3D generation code that produced the training questions (`docs/prior_art/vla_3d.md`, verified against the organizers' own `generate_scene_info.py`), and `on` + `near` alone govern 81 relation occurrences across the 75 training questions (`docs/question_analysis.md` §3). These are scoring bugs, not style choices — the GT battery's numerical over-counts (e.g. `home_building_2` red pillows: pipeline 10 vs independent 4) are the predicted signature of the `on` z-band mismatch compounded by the attribute-relaxation fallback (§B-1). The pending k-fold sweep (`docs/cvsweep_rerun_brief.md`) can tune coefficients but cannot discover a different functional form, so the form fixes below must be pinned *before* the sweep reruns; coefficients on the new forms are then the sweep's job.
